Standard Detail: Ditch & Water Descent Connections

Drawing Ref: T2019-323-DD-MD-MDDT-2200-DW-10003-04-A

📐 Full Corridor Cross-Section (DW-10003): Crest Ditch (T11) ── Cut Ditch (T1) ── Track CL ── Shoulder (T9) → Water Descent → Bench (T8) → Dissipator → Toe (T7/4) + Riprap ── Outer Channel (Type B/C)

1. Water Descents

Water descents are transverse slope cascades (DW-10003), not an independent ditch type. They can only connect to half-round ditches (Type 9 at the platform shoulder, Type 8 on intermediate benches) to convey runoff safely down slopes.

2. Transverse Hierarchy

Crest: Type 11 / 5 (top of cut slope).
Formation: Type 1 (cut) / Type 9 (shoulder fill).
Berm: Type 8 Bench ditch (fills > 8m).
Toe: Dissipator + Type 7/4 Ditch + Riprap.
Outermost: Channels A–C & Diversions.

3. Energy Dissipator Basins

Standard fixed point basins (1.2m × 1.8m × 0.4m drop per DW-10002/DW-10003) positioned strictly as point structures at ditch/channel outfalls and at the base of water descents entering toe ditches.

4. Embankment Riprap Armor

Continuous longitudinal stone armor ($D_{50}=100\text{--}200\text{mm}$) placed along erodible toes/slopes over a span (DW-10004); never represented as cross-drainage structures.
